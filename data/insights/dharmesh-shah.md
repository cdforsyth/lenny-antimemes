- **Implementation Antimeme:** You can't systematize charisma.

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Use flash tags for communication clarity** - #FYI vs #suggestion vs #plea
2. **Measure what seems unmeasurable** - LPM for presentations
3. **Build constraints against complexity** - One feature in, one feature out

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Entropy requires constant resistance** - Simplicity isn't one-time
2. **Building broadly can be the right zig** - Integration is the real problem
3. **Zero direct reports can work for 18 years** - Don't fix weaknesses, avoid them

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Dharmesh's antimemes share a structure: **the conventional startup advice (focus, fix weaknesses, trust context for communication) often misses the actual problem (integration pain, strength leverage, communication ambiguity)—and the counterintuitive moves (building broadly, avoiding management, explicit labeling) solve the real problems that conventional wisdom doesn't see**. Measure laughs per minute. Resist entropy constantly. Label your communication intent. The pattern reveals that engineering thinking applied to "soft" domains (humor, culture, communication) produces surprising results, and that contrarian positioning often works because conventional wisdom solves the wrong problem.
