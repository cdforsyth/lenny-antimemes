**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **System Antimeme:** Big products require big plans.
- **Narrative Antimeme:** Success comes from strategy.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Plan before building.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Big plans are valued
- Strategy is assumed

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

2. **$20 pricing was improvised** - Google Form top result
3. **Velocity beats polish in AI** - Ship raw for faster learning

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Nick's antimemes share a structure: **building transformative AI products requires accepting that accidents and speed outperform plans and polish—ChatGPT emerged from accident not strategy as a 10-day hackathon, the $20 pricing was improvised from a Google Form blog post, no waitlist was strategically crucial for real-time emergent use case observation, and velocity beats polish because you won't know what to polish until after you ship**. Accept accidents. Improvise pricing. Skip waitlists. Ship raw. The pattern reveals that building ChatGPT taught that AI product success comes from inversions that feel reckless—where accident beats strategy, improvisation beats research, openness beats gating, and raw beats polished.
