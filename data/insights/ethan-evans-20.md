### Pattern Across Antimemes

Ethan's antimemes share a structure: **career advancement requires embracing beliefs that feel dangerously naive—very few realize significant failures are recoverable, invention is learnable through systematic practice not innate talent, remote work has more improvement potential than established office models, and trust-based relationships outperform contracts**. Recover boldly from failure. Practice invention systematically. Optimize remote work. Build on trust. The pattern reveals that Amazon-level career success came from beliefs that seem professionally reckless—where embracing failure recovery, systematic creativity, new work models, and trust over contracts creates advantages precisely because most people won't risk them.
