## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. PLG Is Actually DLG (Data-Led Growth)

**INSIGHT:** "PLG is actually fundamentally DLG, data led growth." Organizations must collect usage behavior data to understand which features correlate with conversion and retention. The growth isn't product-led in isolation - it's data-led with product as the delivery mechanism.

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Missing
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Vertigo

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 4
- Recognition: 2
- Implication: 5
- Specificity: 4

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Epistemic / Cognitive

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Epistemic Antimeme:** PLG emphasizes product, not data.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** "Product-led" centers the product.
- **Social Antimeme:** PLG is trendier than "data-led."

**Why it resists spreading:**
- PLG is the established term
- Data infrastructure isn't as exciting
- Product focus is the selling point
- DLG reframe challenges PLG identity

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that PLG is primarily about product
- The belief that great product automatically grows
- The comfort of product-centric thinking

**Verification:**
> "PLG is actually fundamentally DLG, data led growth."

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Start with activation** - Often yields retention benefits too
2. **Don't confuse free trial with PLG** - Full investment required
3. **Plan cross-functional change** - Sales, marketing, product all affected

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **PLG is really DLG** - Data infrastructure is foundational
2. **Activation and retention are connected** - Not separate problems
3. **Free trial isn't PLG** - Substantial gap between them

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Hila's antimemes share a structure: **PLG is commonly underspecified - the label gets applied to simple tactics (free trial) when the reality requires deeper foundations (data infrastructure, organizational change, specialized expertise)**. The DLG reframe is the clearest: calling it "product-led" obscures that data is the actual foundation. The pattern reveals that PLG discourse often stops at the tactics visible to users (free trial, self-serve) while ignoring the organizational infrastructure required to make those tactics work.
