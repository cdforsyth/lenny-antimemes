## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. Test Experiences Before Defining Metrics

**INSIGHT:** "Test product experiences before defining formal evaluation metrics." Experience the product first, then figure out what to measure—not the other way around.

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**QUICK TEST:**

- Missing vs. ignoring? Missing
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Recognition

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 5
- Recognition: 3
- Implication: 4
- Specificity: 5

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** System / Epistemic

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 8

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **System Antimeme:** Define success metrics before building.
- **Epistemic Antimeme:** Data-driven means metrics-first.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** You can't evaluate without criteria.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Metrics-first is the standard process
- Data-driven culture is celebrated
- Experience-first seems subjective
- The reversal feels unrigorous

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that metrics should come first
- The belief that evaluation requires predefined criteria
- The comfort of process-driven development

**Verification:**
> "Test product experiences before defining formal evaluation metrics."

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Ask "What would we build if founding today?"** - Reset without starting fresh
2. **Test experiences before defining metrics** - Reverse the order
3. **Build cross-functional baseline competency** - Adjacent discipline knowledge

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Experience before metrics** - Flip the data-driven process
2. **Founding-today question** - Challenge current product baseline
3. **Dual velocity structure** - Fast and slow thinking teams

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Howie's antimemes share a structure: **AI-era product development requires reversing familiar processes (experience before metrics, founding-today question, dual velocities) because the pace of change makes traditional approaches too slow and the new capabilities require hands-on experimentation before systematic evaluation**. Test before measuring. Ask what you'd build today. Create separate speeds. The pattern reveals that the AI paradigm shift requires not just new tools but new operating rhythms—and that the careful, metrics-first, unified-process approaches that worked before now create drag.
