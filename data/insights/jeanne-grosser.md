## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Message pain avoidance, not upside** - 80% buy to reduce risk
2. **Consider GTM engineering** - One engineer vs. 10 SDRs
3. **Design GTM as product** - Not as operations

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Sales experience will differentiate** - As products converge
2. **80% buy to avoid pain** - Not pursue upside
3. **$1K engineer replaced $1M SDRs** - GTM automation reality

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Jeanne's antimemes share a structure: **go-to-market effectiveness requires inverting the assumptions that sales and marketing teams hold—being sold to will differentiate companies when products converge, 80% of customers buy to avoid pain not pursue upside, one GTM engineer replaced $1M in SDR salaries at Vercel, and GTM should be designed as deliberately as product**. Differentiate through sales experience. Message pain avoidance. Automate with engineers. Design GTM as product. The pattern reveals that Stripe's GTM philosophy treats customer acquisition as product design—where the sales experience, pain messaging, engineering automation, and deliberate journey design replace the traditional product focus, upside messaging, headcount scaling, and operational GTM that most companies default to.
