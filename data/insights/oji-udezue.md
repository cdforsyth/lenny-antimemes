## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. Framework Blindness Kills Insight

**INSIGHT:** "Product leaders who over-rely on frameworks miss context-specific insights. Frameworks are starting points, not answers. The best PMs know when to abandon the framework because the situation demands something the framework can't see."

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Frameworks provide answers.
- **System Antimeme:** Structured thinking is rigorous.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Apply frameworks consistently.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Frameworks feel rigorous
- Structure is valued
- Consistency is taught
- The blindness is hidden

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that frameworks provide answers
- The belief that structure ensures rigor

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Abandon frameworks when context demands** - They're starting points
2. **Build organic virality through value** - Not mechanics
3. **Go vertical before horizontal** - Deep before wide

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Framework blindness kills insight** - Over-reliance misses context
2. **Synthetic virality caps growth** - Engineered sharing has limits
3. **PLG requires sales anyway** - Self-serve hits ceilings

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Oji's antimemes share a structure: **PLG product leadership requires accepting counterintuitive truths about frameworks, virality, and sales—framework blindness kills insight because over-reliance misses context-specific understanding, synthetic virality caps growth because engineered sharing hits ceilings organic doesn't, horizontal products need vertical entry to resonate initially, and PLG requires sales anyway because self-serve hits ceilings**. Abandon frameworks. Build organic value. Go vertical first. Layer sales. The pattern reveals that building Calendly and Typeform taught that PLG success comes from inversions that feel like betrayal—where frameworks are abandoned, mechanics are avoided, horizontal goes vertical, and PLG adds sales.
