- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Agents can automate immediately.

**Why it resists spreading:**

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Plan for 4-6 months integration** - Not "one-click"
2. **Rebuild AI intuitions monthly** - Knowledge decays fast
3. **Invest in workflow understanding** - Over flashy AI solutions

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **"One-click agents" are marketing fiction** - 4-6 months is reality
2. **Judgment beats implementation** - As build costs plummet
3. **AI knowledge must be refreshed monthly** - Intuitions expire

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Aishwarya and Kiriti's antimemes share a structure: **AI product success requires embracing the slow, judgment-heavy work that contradicts AI's fast-and-automated promise—"one-click agents" are pure marketing while enterprises need 4-6 months, leadership must rebuild AI intuitions monthly as capabilities change, deep workflow understanding beats flashy technical solutions, and design judgment becomes the differentiator as implementation costs plummet**. Ignore the marketing. Learn continuously. Understand workflows. Develop judgment. The pattern reveals that AI products succeed through the human elements that seem counter to AI's value proposition—where patience, continuous learning, problem understanding, and design judgment matter more than the technical automation that AI promises to deliver.
