## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Maintain team stability for years** - Before expecting breakout
2. **Track model capability as constraint** - Not just product-market fit
3. **Increase context density, not headcount** - Alternative to hiring

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **PMs and designers as primary value creators** - Not engineers
2. **Model timing was the constraint** - Not founder execution
3. **Context density over headcount** - 15 people, million users

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Eric's antimemes share a structure: **hypergrowth success stories hide the actual mechanisms—Bolt's overnight success came from seven years of team stability waiting for model capabilities to mature, scaling happened through context density rather than hiring, and the emerging AI paradigm shifts value creation from engineers to PMs and designers**. Wait for model timing. Keep the team together. Don't hire to scale. Elevate PM/design. The pattern reveals that Bolt's story inverts conventional startup wisdom—where the breakthrough came from external timing rather than execution, scale from density rather than headcount, and value from product thinking rather than engineering.
