**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 8

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Organizational Antimeme:** Engineers know their timelines.
- **Social Antimeme:** Challenging estimates creates conflict.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Trust the experts.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Challenging estimates feels micromanaging
- Engineers resist timeline pressure

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Dylan's antimemes share a structure: **sustainable product excellence requires the counterintuitive, slow, and rigorous work that speed-obsessed cultures resist—challenging timeline assumptions reveals hidden padding, "fun" proved to be FigJam's differentiator despite B2B conventions, AI feature failures were QA problems not technology limitations, and design craft becomes survival strategy as AI accelerates software creation**. Question estimates. Embrace fun. Apply QA rigor. Invest in craft. The pattern reveals that 13-year-old Figma maintains velocity not through the obvious speed tactics but through the counterintuitive investments in rigor, craft, and questioning assumptions that seem to slow things down but actually enable sustainable speed.
