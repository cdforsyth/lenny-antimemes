## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Don't build workarounds for model limits** - They'll vanish
2. **Use multiple models for different tasks** - Ensemble architecture
3. **Look at regulatory, not technical barriers** - For AI applications

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Ambiguity is competitive advantage** - Constant upheaval
2. **Model maximalism beats scaffolding** - Don't defend against current limits
3. **Chat's fuzziness is the feature** - Unpredictability works

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Kevin's antimemes share a structure: **building AI products at OpenAI requires embracing what feels like chaos—ambiguity is competitive advantage because constant strategy rethinking beats stable plans, model maximalism beats scaffolding because workarounds for current limits become waste, ensemble architecture with multiple specialized models mirrors organizational structure, and the barriers to AI education are regulatory not technical**. Embrace ambiguity. Skip workarounds. Use model ensembles. Look past technical barriers. The pattern reveals that OpenAI's CPO sees AI product development requiring the opposite of traditional software discipline—where chaos beats clarity, maximalism beats defense, complexity beats simplicity, and regulatory beats technical as the real barrier.
