**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Temporal Antimeme:** Product-led means faster sales.
- **System Antimeme:** Self-serve shortens cycles.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Usage signals immediate readiness.

**Why it resists spreading:**

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Don't contact new users immediately** - Against all sales instinct
2. **Marketing must find decision-makers** - Users aren't buyers
3. **Validate signals manually first** - Before automating

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **12+ months to enterprise deal** - PLG isn't faster
2. **Product owns pipeline KPIs** - Not sales's job alone
3. **Users and buyers are different people** - Bottom-up has a ceiling

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Elena's PLS antimemes share a structure: **product-led sales requires abandoning the assumptions that made PLG attractive—12+ months from signup to enterprise deal contradicts the speed promise, don't contact new users immediately defies sales training, most users aren't buyers means bottom-up alone won't work, and product must own pipeline KPIs crosses organizational boundaries**. Expect long timelines. Resist quick contact. Find the actual buyer. Make product own revenue. The pattern reveals that product-led sales isn't a faster or easier path to enterprise revenue—it's a fundamentally different motion that requires patience, restraint, cross-functional ownership, and accepting that the product signal is the beginning, not the end, of the sales process.
