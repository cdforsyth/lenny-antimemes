## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Start with development plans, not frameworks** - Momentum before completeness
2. **Invest two weeks in strategy storytelling** - Substantial time, not hours
3. **Prepare multiple story formats** - 75-second, 6-minute, extended

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Storytelling as career limiting factor** - Not optional for advancement
2. **Traits vs skills differ in teachability** - Not everything develops equally
3. **Two weeks for quarterly communication** - Time investment far exceeds expectations

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Petra's antimemes share a structure: **the time and deliberateness required for PM development and communication far exceeds typical investment—and underinvestment in storytelling specifically limits careers**. Two weeks for strategy stories. Multiple prepared formats. Development before frameworks. The pattern reveals that PM coaching and communication effectiveness comes from substantially more preparation than most people allocate, and that storytelling isn't supplementary to PM work—it's career-determining.
