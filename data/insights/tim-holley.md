On the other hand, not fast. Etsy discovered the opposite—their consensus culture created bottlenecks that prevented rapid iteration."

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Replace consensus with single PM accountability** - Speed over debate
2. **Expect 80% experiment failure** - Learning not winning
3. **Focus on one side to compound the other** - Sellers become buyers

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Consensus culture was a liability** - Created bottlenecks
2. **80% failure rate is expected and healthy** - Learning mechanism
3. **One-line copy changes outperform features** - Values beat complexity

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Tim's antimemes share a structure: **marketplace product leadership requires accepting counterintuitive truths about collaboration, failure, and impact—consensus culture was actually a liability because it created bottlenecks, 80% experiment failure rate is expected and healthy because experimentation is learning, sellers drive buyer acquisition better than marketing because one side compounds the other, and one-line copy changes can outperform major features because values alignment beats complexity**. Replace consensus. Expect failure. Focus one side. Try copy changes. The pattern reveals that building Etsy taught that marketplace success comes from inversions that feel wrong—where consensus hurts, failure is normal, one side drives both, and copy beats features.
