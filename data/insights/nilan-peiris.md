## Stage 1: Popular vs. Rare Concepts

2. **Growth requires product-market fit** - Get the basics right

## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. NPS Doubling Creates Exponential Growth

**INSIGHT:** "We found that every 10-point increase in NPS roughly doubled word-of-mouth referrals. This isn't linear—it's exponential. Most companies treat NPS as a vanity metric instead of the growth lever it actually is."

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Missing
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Vertigo

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 5
- Recognition: 2
- Implication: 5
- Specificity: 5

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** System / Cognitive

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **System Antimeme:** NPS is a vanity metric.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Growth comes from marketing.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Invest in acquisition.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- NPS seems like vanity
- Marketing is the lever
- Acquisition is prioritized
- The exponential relationship is missed

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that NPS is secondary
- The belief that marketing drives growth
- The comfort of acquisition focus

**Verification:**
> We found that every 10-point increase in NPS roughly doubled word-of-mouth referrals. This isn't linear—it's exponential. Most companies treat NPS as a vanity metric instead of the growth lever it actually is.

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Treat NPS as growth lever** - 10 points doubles word-of-mouth
2. **Focus on product not referral program** - Program is symptom
3. **Remove features to grow** - Subtraction beats addition

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **NPS doubling creates exponential growth** - Not a vanity metric
2. **Referral programs are symptoms not causes** - Product drives referrals
3. **Price transparency as moat** - Show competitor pricing

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Nilan's antimemes share a structure: **fintech growth requires accepting counterintuitive truths about metrics, programs, and transparency—NPS doubling creates exponential growth because each 10-point increase doubles word-of-mouth, referral programs are symptoms not causes because product drives referrals, growth team should shrink product because removing features improves conversion, and price transparency is a moat because showing competitors' fees is the product**. Invest in NPS. Ignore program mechanics. Remove features. Show competitors. The pattern reveals that building Wise taught that fintech growth comes from inversions that feel risky—where NPS drives growth, product beats programs, subtraction beats addition, and transparency beats hiding.
