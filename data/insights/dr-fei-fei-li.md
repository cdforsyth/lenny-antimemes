2. **The bitter lesson has limits** - 2D data can't train 3D robots
3. **ImageNet succeeded through audacity** - Grad students, not corporations

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Dr. Fei-Fei Li's antimemes share a structure: **AI research requires recognizing that universal assumptions have hidden limits—the AI label went from dirty word to mandatory branding in 18 months, the bitter lesson of scale has limits because robotics lacks 3D action data, robots are physical systems with 20+ year timelines despite software progress, and ImageNet succeeded through audacity not capital because data scarcity was the real bottleneck**. Remember AI's recent stigma. Question scale assumptions. Expect longer robot timelines. Try audacity over resources. The pattern reveals that pioneering AI research taught that breakthroughs come from recognizing where conventional wisdom fails—where labels flip, scale fails, timelines extend, and audacity beats funding.
