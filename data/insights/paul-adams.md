## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Follow skeptics deliberately** - Counter optimism bias with contrary reading
2. **Watch for pendulum overcorrection** - Awareness prevents swinging too far
3. **Restructure around AI, don't bolt on** - True transformation requires rebuild

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Public failure as formative** - Catastrophic stage freeze became pivotal
2. **AI restructuring vs bolting on** - Adding features isn't transformation
3. **Invisible displacement through reduced backfill** - No firings, just fewer hires

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Paul's antimemes share a structure: **the visible framing of change (AI adds features, failure is hidden, jobs are lost through layoffs, strategy shifts are bold) misses the actual mechanism (AI requires restructuring, failure is formative, displacement is invisible backfill reduction, shifts overcorrect)**. The real dynamics are less dramatic but more accurate. The pattern reveals that understanding product and organizational change requires looking past the obvious framing to see the actual mechanisms at work.
