Sander's antimemes share a structure: **AI security requires accepting counterintuitive truths about protection, statistics, and expertise—guardrails create false security rather than protection by generating dangerous overconfidence, the attack surface is essentially infinite making 99% defense rates meaningless, traditional cybersecurity paradigms don't apply to AI because you can't patch a brain, and damage hasn't occurred due to limited deployment not security success**.
