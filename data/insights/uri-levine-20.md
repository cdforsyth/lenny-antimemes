## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Prepare for luck, don't blame it** - Readiness is controllable
2. **Accept responsibility regardless of fault** - Control your destiny
3. **Decide fast in crisis** - Waiting erodes options exponentially

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Luck is opportunity meeting readiness** - Not random destiny
2. **Responsibility is not fault** - Accept control regardless of cause
3. **PMF loss masquerades as business failure** - Market shift vs execution

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Uri's antimemes share a structure: **crisis management requires accepting counterintuitive truths about luck, responsibility, and speed—luck is opportunity meeting readiness not random destiny because preparation is controllable, responsibility not fault wins companies because accepting control regardless of cause outperforms excuses, speed of decision compounds options exponentially because waiting erodes runway faster than crisis, and product-market fit loss masquerades as business failure because market shifts aren't execution problems**. Prepare for luck. Accept responsibility. Decide fast. Diagnose market shifts. The pattern reveals that founding Waze and advising founders taught that crisis survival comes from inversions that feel unfair—where luck is earned, responsibility is voluntary, speed beats deliberation, and failure isn't always fixable.
