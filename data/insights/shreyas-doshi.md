## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Differentiate task types (LNO)** - Don't treat all tasks identically
2. **Track repeated bandaids** - Pattern indicates strategy/culture problem
3. **Prioritize opportunity cost over ROI** - Best use, not just positive return

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Execution problems are strategy/culture problems** - Wrong category entirely
2. **Ambitious people's fatal error** - Uniform effort wastes leverage
3. **Opportunity cost over ROI** - Different optimization target

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Shreyas's antimemes share a structure: **the visible, actionable level of product work (execution, quick wins, uniform effort) often distracts from the level where leverage actually exists (strategy, opportunity cost, differentiated effort)**. Execution problems are strategy problems. ROI work misses opportunity cost. Treating all tasks equally wastes leverage. The pattern reveals that PM effectiveness comes from operating at a different level than the obvious one—seeing through execution to strategy, through ROI to opportunity cost, through uniform effort to leverage.
