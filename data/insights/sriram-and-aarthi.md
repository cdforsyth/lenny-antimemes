## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. Jobs-to-be-Done Framework Is Fundamentally Flawed

**INSIGHT:** "JTBD oversimplifies product reality by ignoring multi-stakeholder systems and competitive trade-offs. Real products must serve conflicting interests simultaneously—Facebook made user experience worse to help new signups reach their 10-friends-in-14-days milestone. This was performing no job for you, it was trying to perform a job for them."

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Missing
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Vertigo

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 5
- Recognition: 2
- Implication: 5
- Specificity: 5

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** System / Cognitive

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **System Antimeme:** JTBD is a complete framework.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Users hire products for jobs.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Apply JTBD to product decisions.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- JTBD is popular
- Framework is trusted
- Application is standard
- The multi-stakeholder complexity is missed

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that JTBD is complete
- The belief that users hire products simply
- The comfort of framework application

**Verification:**
> "This was performing no job for you, it was trying to perform a job for them."

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Create content now, not after achievements** - Reps teach what works
2. **Use imposter syndrome as growth signal** - Retreat to mastery, expand out
3. **Recognize JTBD limitations** - Multi-stakeholder reality

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **JTBD framework is fundamentally flawed** - Ignores conflicting interests
2. **Everyone should create content immediately** - Don't wait for credentials
3. **Market forces trump strategy and effort** - Execution can't overcome conditions

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Sriram and Aarthi's antimemes share a structure: **career and product success requires accepting counterintuitive truths about frameworks, credentials, and effort—Jobs-to-be-Done is fundamentally flawed because it ignores multi-stakeholder systems and conflicting interests, everyone should create content immediately because building reps matters more than credentials, imposter syndrome signals you're on the right path because it's evidence of growth, and market forces trump strategy and effort because superior execution can't overcome unfavorable conditions**. Question JTBD. Create now. Embrace imposter feelings. Respect market forces. The pattern reveals that building products at Microsoft, Facebook, and Netflix taught that professional success comes from inversions that feel uncomfortable—where frameworks fail, credentials don't matter, imposter is signal, and markets beat execution.
