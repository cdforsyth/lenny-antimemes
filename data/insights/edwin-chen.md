## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Build the one thing only you could build** - Don't pivot
2. **Consider bootstrapping** - VC isn't required for scale
3. **Invest in human judgment** - Essential, not transitional

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Industry optimizing for slop** - Benchmarks don't measure progress
2. **$1B+ without VC** - Playbook isn't required
3. **Dopamine over truth** - Gaming metrics, not advancing

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Edwin's antimemes share a structure: **AI industry success requires rejecting the industry's own metrics and paths—benchmark optimization produces "AI slop" rather than meaningful progress, the Silicon Valley playbook of VC and pivots isn't necessary for billion-dollar outcomes, building one thing only you could build beats iterating toward product-market fit, and human judgment remains essential rather than being a transition state**. Ignore benchmarks. Skip VC. Don't pivot. Keep humans. The pattern reveals that success in AI comes from being contrarian about AI—where the metrics, funding models, and automation narratives the industry celebrates are the things to avoid.
