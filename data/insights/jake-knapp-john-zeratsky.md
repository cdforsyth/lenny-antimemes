## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Redesign environment, not willpower** - Delete apps, log out, cancel services
2. **Recognize busyness as performance** - Social pressure, not necessity
3. **Choose one highlight daily** - Intentionality over optimization

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **"It's not about productivity"** - Intentionality over output
2. **Busy bandwagon is social pressure** - Not personal failure
3. **Infinity pools are structural** - Environmental design required

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Jake and John's antimemes share a structure: **attention and focus require abandoning the personal responsibility narrative—"it's not about productivity" reframes the entire category, the "busy bandwagon" is social pressure not personal failure, "infinity pools" are structural problems not discipline failures, and willpower doesn't work while environmental design does**. Forget productivity. See busyness as performance. Treat distraction structurally. Design your environment. The pattern reveals that the "Make Time" framework's power comes from rejecting the premises of its own category—where productivity optimization, personal discipline, and willpower-based solutions are the problems, not the solutions, and the real work is environmental and structural rather than personal and effortful.
