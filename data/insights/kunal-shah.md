## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Diversify in low-ARPU markets** - Focus is a curse
2. **Design for different time values** - Hourly wages shaped West
3. **Consider status signaling in pricing** - Waste can be a feature

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Focus is a curse in Asia** - Portfolio required
2. **Inefficiency is largest employer** - Efficiency destroys jobs
3. **Status through waste** - Expensive beats efficient

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Kunal's antimemes share a structure: **building in emerging markets requires inverting Western business wisdom—focus is a curse in Asia because low ARPUs require portfolio approaches, hourly wage absence creates fundamentally different time valuation, inefficiency is the world's largest employer and AI threatens destabilization, and status comes through waste not efficiency as seen in Apple vs Meta pricing**. Diversify, don't focus. Design for different time values. Preserve some inefficiency. Price for status, not utility. The pattern reveals that building CRED in India taught that Western startup orthodoxy inverts in developing markets—where focus fails, time works differently, inefficiency serves a purpose, and waste signals status.
