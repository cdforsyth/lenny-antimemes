## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **One interview beats zero** - Start small rather than waiting
2. **Interview conversationally** - Beer with buddy, not formal protocol
3. **Run discovery parallel to delivery** - Not sequential phases

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **98% write opportunities as solutions** - Almost everyone does it wrong
2. **Most decisions use zero customer data** - The real baseline
3. **Hypotheticals don't predict behavior** - Can't trust stated intentions

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Teresa's antimemes share a structure: **the formalized, "rigorous" approach to product discovery (formal protocols, sequential phases, statistical samples, hypothetical questions) often produces worse outcomes than informal, continuous, small-scale actual behavior research**. Casual interviews beat protocols. One interview beats zero. Parallel beats sequential. The pattern reveals that discovery effectiveness comes from frequency and behavioral focus, not formality and sample size—and 98% of teams are doing it wrong from the start by confusing opportunities with solutions.
