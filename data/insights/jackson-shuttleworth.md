## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Ensure core product is valuable first** - Gamification is a hack
2. **Try the simplest notification timing** - 23.5 hours beat ML
3. **Show you care about the feature** - Users mirror company emphasis

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Streaks need underlying value** - Gamification isn't the answer
2. **Simple heuristics beat algorithms** - 23.5 hours outperformed ML
3. **Company emphasis creates user value** - Visibility drives caring

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Jackson's antimemes share a structure: **retention success requires the counterintuitive simplicity that sophisticated product teams resist—streaks are an engagement hack that requires underlying product value, 23.5-hour notification timing beat complex ML algorithms, users care about streaks because Duolingo visibly cares about them, and clear communication beats feature complexity**. Build value first. Try simple signals. Show you care. Communicate clearly. The pattern reveals that Duolingo's 600+ experiments led to simpler, more psychological insights—where underlying value, basic heuristics, company emphasis, and communication clarity outperformed the sophisticated features, algorithms, and engineering complexity that product teams instinctively build.
