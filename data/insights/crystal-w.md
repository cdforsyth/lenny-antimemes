## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. Sample Size of 30 Suffices for Directional Insights

**INSIGHT:** "Mathematically speaking, you get the same level of trends with 30 data points versus 100—only precision improves, not underlying patterns."

**CLASSIFICATION:** Diagnostic

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y

- Recognition vs. vertigo? Recognition

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 5
- Recognition: 3
- Implication: 4
- Specificity: 5

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** System / Cognitive

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 8

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **System Antimeme:** Statistical significance requires scale.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** More data means better insights.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Wait for sufficient sample.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Statistical significance is taught
- More data is valued
- Waiting seems responsible
- The threshold is overestimated

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that scale is required
- The belief that more data is always better
- The comfort of waiting for significance

**Verification:**
> Teams delay experiments waiting for statistical significance. Widjaja reveals that "mathematically speaking, you get the same level of trends" with 30 data points versus 100—only precision improves, not underlying patterns.

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Test with 30 data points** - Directional insights don't need scale
2. **Fix copy before features** - Messaging often beats engineering
3. **Instrument with properties** - Context makes data actionable

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Sample size of 30 suffices** - Same trends, less precision
2. **Super apps emerged from storage constraints** - Hardware, not strategy
3. **Analytics failures are instrumentation** - Not tools

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Crystal's antimemes share a structure: **growth and analytics work requires recognizing that the real blockers are upstream and hidden—sample size of 30 suffices because trends appear before precision, copy often beats product features because messaging is the blocker not functionality, super apps emerged due to phone storage constraints not strategic innovation, and analytics failures root in instrumentation not visualization tools**. Test with 30. Fix messaging. Understand constraints. Instrument properly. The pattern reveals that growth leadership at Gojek taught that effectiveness comes from finding the hidden upstream causes—where small samples work, copy beats code, hardware explains strategy, and capture beats dashboards.
