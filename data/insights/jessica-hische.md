## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. Non-Designers Understand Design Better Than They Think

**INSIGHT:** "Most people instinctively understand design principles better than they realize...what we are as people are endless absorbers of patterns." Non-designers should trust their intuitive reactions.

**SCORES:**

- Implication: 5
- Specificity: 5

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Identity / Cognitive

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Identity Antimeme:** Design requires expertise.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Intuition isn't trustworthy.
- **System Antimeme:** Professionals know better.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Design expertise is a profession
- Intuition seems unreliable
- "Trust your gut" sounds unrigorous
- Pattern absorption isn't conscious

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that design requires expertise
- The belief that intuition should be doubted
- The comfort of deferring to professionals

**Verification:**
> "What we are as people are endless absorbers of patterns."

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Trust your design intuition** - You absorb patterns constantly
2. **Revisit earlier directions** - Later paths validate instincts
3. **Do pragmatic refreshes** - Not wholesale rebrands

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Non-designers understand design** - Pattern absorption is universal
2. **Going back can move forward** - Non-linear process works
3. **Diversification prevents burnout** - Not dilution

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Jessica's antimemes share a structure: **creative excellence requires trusting the intuitive, non-linear, pragmatic approaches that professional culture dismisses—non-designers understand design better than they think through pattern absorption, walking one path sometimes validates earlier instincts rather than being regression, pragmatic refreshes work better than wholesale rebrands, and multiple creative domains prevent burnout rather than diluting focus**. Trust your design intuition. Revisit early directions. Refresh pragmatically. Diversify creatively. The pattern reveals that professional design work benefits from the approaches that seem amateur—where intuition, non-linear process, incremental updates, and domain variety outperform the expertise deference, forward-only iteration, complete rebrands, and specialization that professional culture promotes.
