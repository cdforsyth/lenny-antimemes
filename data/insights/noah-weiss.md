## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. Each Customer Cohort Requires New PMF

**INSIGHT:** "Each customer cohort requires establishing product-market fit anew—not a one-time achievement." PMF isn't something you achieve once; expanding to new segments means finding fit again from scratch.

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Missing
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Vertigo

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 4
- Recognition: 2
- Implication: 5
- Specificity: 4

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Epistemic / Cognitive

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Epistemic Antimeme:** PMF is a milestone to achieve.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Expansion should be easier after PMF.
- **System Antimeme:** Growth playbooks assume PMF transfers.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- "We have PMF" is a celebration
- New cohorts should be easier
- The re-finding process isn't taught
- It contradicts growth narratives

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that PMF is achieved once
- The belief that expansion leverages existing fit
- The comfort of PMF as a destination

**Verification:**
> "Each customer cohort requires establishing product-market fit anew—not a one-time achievement."

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Match UI confidence to data quality** - Don't project false certainty
2. **Measure successful teams, not users** - Better predictor of value
3. **Counter power user blindness** - Fresh eyes reveal what familiarity hides

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **PMF must be found for each cohort** - Not a one-time achievement
2. **Sacrifice short-term metrics for learning** - Trade impact for insight
3. **Successful teams over user count** - Different metric, different strategy

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Noah's antimemes share a structure: **the standard success metrics and practices (user counts, metric improvement, product familiarity, confident UI) can mislead when entering new territory—and the fix requires deliberately trading visible success for less measurable learning**. PMF must be re-established per cohort. Metrics should be sacrificed for learning. Power users become blind. The pattern reveals that growth-phase product work requires accepting temporary metric regression and uncertainty that goes against standard performance evaluation.
