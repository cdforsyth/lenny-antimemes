### Pattern Across Antimemes

Yuhki's antimemes share a structure: **product leadership at Figma requires accepting counterintuitive truths about ownership, management, and signals—PMs should own the why not the what because rationale is the unique responsibility while solutions emerge from collaboration, managing designers is harder than managing PMs because craft growth doesn't align with company priorities, one concerning tweet with zero likes can signal deeper patterns because authentic friction matters more than viral validation, and motivation cuts implementation time in half because ownership unlocks velocity that pressure cannot**. Own the why. Expect design challenges. Track friction. Create ownership. The pattern reveals that leading product at Figma taught that effectiveness comes from inversions that feel like abdication—where why beats what, design is harder, friction beats engagement, and ownership beats pressure.
