## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Ship worse products** - Imperfect beats delayed
2. **Consider consulting first** - Two years of learning
3. **Price for small fraction of value** - Not maximization

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **"Worse is better"** - Against quality obsession
2. **Tech debt indicates success** - Champagne problem
3. **Two years consulting before product** - Patience over speed

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Julia's antimemes share a structure: **dbt's success came from the patient, imperfect, generous approaches that contradict startup wisdom—"worse is better" means shipping imperfect products over polished ones, tech debt is a champagne problem indicating user adoption rather than engineering failure, two years consulting before productizing built deep understanding, and pricing captures only a small fraction of value created**. Ship worse. Celebrate tech debt. Do consulting first. Charge less. The pattern reveals that developer tool success came from reversing conventional startup metrics—where imperfect products, accumulated debt, services-first, and underpricing created the adoption and understanding that perfectionism, clean code, product-first, and value capture maximization would have prevented.
