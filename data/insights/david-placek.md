- **System Antimeme:** Brief teams thoroughly for best work.

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Disguise briefs from creative teams** - Context harms ideation
2. **Interpret comfort as warning signal** - Discomfort means distinctiveness
3. **Use small teams, not brainstorming** - Large sessions underperform

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Less context produces better creative work** - Disguised briefs win
2. **Comfort means you don't have it** - Inversion of consensus value
3. **Sound has meaning** - Letters evoke specific responses

### Pattern Across Antimemes

David's antimemes share a structure: **effective naming requires inverting creative process assumptions—teams with disguised briefs outperform those with full context, comfort signals failure rather than success, large brainstorming sessions underperform small focused teams, and naming has scientific foundations in sound symbolism that contradict the belief that names are arbitrary**. Hide the brief. Seek discomfort. Shrink the team. Study the sounds. The pattern reveals that professional naming success comes from systematically doing the opposite of standard creative practices—where less information, more discomfort, smaller groups, and scientific rigor produce better results than the inclusive, comfortable, context-rich brainstorming that feels right.
