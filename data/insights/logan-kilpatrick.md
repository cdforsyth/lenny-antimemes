## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Provide detailed context to AI** - Generic yields generic
2. **Explore non-chat UI paradigms** - Major opportunity
3. **Build domain expertise moat** - Not technical AI moat

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Trust enables speed more than process** - Autonomy over consensus
2. **Domain expertise is the defensibility** - Not AI capability
3. **Chat isn't the only paradigm** - Opportunity in new interfaces

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Logan's antimemes share a structure: **AI product success requires the non-obvious investments that seem secondary to the technology—trust enables speed more than process at OpenAI, context determines output quality despite simple prompts feeling natural, new UI paradigms beyond chat are the opportunity despite chat defining the category, and domain expertise creates defensibility despite AI capability seeming like the moat**. Trust over process. Context over simplicity. New UIs over chat. Domain over tech. The pattern reveals that OpenAI's inside perspective inverts common AI product assumptions—where organizational trust, prompt context, interface creativity, and domain expertise matter more than consensus, minimal prompts, chat interfaces, and AI technical capability.
