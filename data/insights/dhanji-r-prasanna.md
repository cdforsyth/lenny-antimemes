## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Question if work is needed** - Elimination beats optimization
2. **Plan for overnight AI work** - Not just interactive assistance
3. **Consider complete rewrites** - AI changes the calculus

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Code quality doesn't predict success** - Zero correlation
2. **AI should work 24/7 autonomously** - Not interactive assistance
3. **Rewrites become viable with AI** - Conventional wisdom inverted

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Dhanji's antimemes share a structure: **AI-era engineering leadership requires abandoning deeply held engineering beliefs—code quality has zero correlation with product success despite being the measure of engineering excellence, AI should work overnight without supervision rather than interactively, questioning whether work is needed yields more than automating it, and complete rewrites become viable despite being engineering's cardinal sin**. Ignore code quality for product decisions. Plan for autonomous overnight AI. Eliminate processes before automating. Consider rewrites. The pattern reveals that a CTO overseeing 3,500 engineers sees AI inverting the foundational beliefs of software engineering—where quality doesn't predict success, assistance becomes autonomy, automation loses to elimination, and rewrites beat iteration.
