### Pattern Across Antimemes

Merci's antimemes share a structure: **product-led growth success requires accepting uncomfortable truths about what actually drives decisions—"the story always won" over metrics at Slack despite data-driven being the standard, passive recruitment produces homogeneous teams despite meritocracy assumptions, onboarding must be intertwined with product despite tutorials being the pattern, and PLG has strict boundary conditions despite being celebrated universally**. Win with story. Recruit actively. Intertwine onboarding. Check PLG fit. The pattern reveals that Slack's growth leadership came from the nuanced, structural, narrative-driven approaches that contradict the simplified, metrics-first, universal PLG playbook that's typically taught.
