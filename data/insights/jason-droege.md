**INSIGHT:** "The 95% POC failure rate cited in research reflects rushed implementations rather than fundamental AI limitations."

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** System / Cognitive

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **System Antimeme:** AI technology has limitations.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** POC failures indicate poor AI fit.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** If it doesn't work, AI isn't ready.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- AI limitations is the accepted narrative
- POC failures are blamed on technology
- Implementation quality isn't measured
- The reframe shifts accountability

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that AI fails due to AI
- The belief that POC results indicate AI capability
- The comfort of blaming technology

**Verification:**
> "The 95% POC failure rate...reflects rushed implementations rather than fundamental AI limitations."

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Ask "why do I have unique insight?"** - Before starting
2. **Use margins as differentiation signal** - Not cost signal
3. **Prioritize not losing** - Before aggressive growth

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **95% POC failure is implementation** - Not AI limitation
2. **Low margins signal differentiation gaps** - Not cost issues
3. **Not losing precedes winning** - Risk over growth

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Jason's antimemes share a structure: **sustainable success requires reframing metrics and priorities that seem obviously about something else—95% AI POC failure reflects rushed implementation not technology limits, low margins signal differentiation gaps not cost problems, "not losing is a precursor to winning" prioritizes survival over growth, and founding requires unique insight not just market opportunity**. Fix implementation, not blame AI. Read margins as differentiation. Survive to win. Have unique insight. The pattern reveals that Scale AI and Uber Eats success came from diagnostic reframing—where the obvious interpretation (AI limits, cost problems, growth priority, market opportunity) masked the real signal (implementation quality, differentiation gaps, survival, unique insight).
