## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Don't redesign your homepage for growth** - Impact is minimal
2. **Don't copy competitors** - 95% failure rate without context
3. **Don't test everything** - Creates paralysis

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Head of growth before PMF fails** - Can't outsource distribution
2. **Copying competitors fails 95%** - Context doesn't transfer
3. **Testing everything paralyzes** - Rigor can hurt

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Elena's growth myth antimemes share a structure: **growth best practices often produce the opposite of their intended effect—hiring growth leaders before PMF fails because distribution can't be outsourced, homepage redesigns rarely drive acquisition despite feeling like the front door, copying competitors fails 95% of the time despite benchmarking being standard practice, and testing everything creates paralysis despite data-driven being the goal**. Don't hire growth early. Skip the homepage redesign. Stop copying competitors. Test less. The pattern reveals that growth effectiveness requires abandoning the standard playbook—where the obvious, defensible, "professional" approaches (hiring experts, improving websites, benchmarking, testing rigorously) often work against growth rather than for it.
