Mayur's antimemes share a structure: **product leadership requires accepting that conventional wisdom about strategy, compensation, and company capabilities is inverted—strategy is overrated because speed beats positioning, early compensation optimization is wrong because 90% comes in the last five years, company DNA predetermines category winners independent of resources, and location arbitrage at early-stage winners creates network density**. Focus on speed. Stop early comp obsession. Recognize structural ceilings. Join early-stage winners. The pattern reveals that product leadership at N26 and Binance taught that career success comes from inversions of standard advice—where speed beats strategy, patience beats negotiation, DNA beats resources, and early-stage beats established.
