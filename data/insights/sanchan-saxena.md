## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. Process Kills Content

**INSIGHT:** "Bring in people who know content and teach them process, as opposed to bringing in people who know process. Most leaders prioritize establishing processes and systems, but the opposite drives success—hiring people who understand what to build and teaching them process."

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Organizational Antimeme:** Process enables scale.
- **System Antimeme:** Hire for process expertise.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Systems first, substance second.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Process is valued
- Process expertise is hired
- Systems come first

**What's lost by accepting it:**

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Hire for content, teach process** - Substance over systems
2. **Commit to vision before testing** - Intentionality beats research
3. **Trust gut as pre-validated data** - Immersion builds intuition

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Process kills content** - Substance people can learn process
2. **A/B testing can damage breakthroughs** - Systrom didn't test Stories
3. **Gut instinct is legitimate data** - Just not statistically significant yet

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Sanchan's antimemes share a structure: **product leadership requires accepting counterintuitive truths about process, testing, and intuition—process kills content because substance people can learn systems but not vice versa, A/B testing can damage breakthrough products because intentionality beats research, gut instinct is legitimate data because it's just not statistically significant yet, and rapid recovery beats perfect planning because two-week cycles outperform scenario anticipation**. Hire for substance. Commit before testing. Trust immersion-built intuition. Build recovery skills. The pattern reveals that leading product at Instagram and Airbnb taught that breakthrough success comes from inversions that feel reckless—where content beats process, vision beats testing, gut beats data, and recovery beats planning.
