## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Get cited across Reddit, YouTube, affiliates** - Not just your site
2. **Run experiments on SEO practices** - Most are unverified
3. **Edit AI-generated content** - Unedited doesn't work

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Early-stage companies can win AEO quickly** - No domain authority needed
2. **Most SEO best practices are unverified** - Validate everything
3. **AI content without editing fails** - Human touch required

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Ethan's antimemes share a structure: **AEO success requires abandoning SEO assumptions—early-stage companies can win without domain authority since citation matters more than ranking, most SEO best practices are unverified despite being presented as established, AI-generated content fails without human editing despite scale being the promised advantage, and citations across Reddit and YouTube matter more than on-site optimization**. Win without authority. Verify the practices. Edit AI content. Get cited everywhere. The pattern reveals that AEO inverts traditional SEO—where the time-based moats, established practices, AI shortcuts, and on-site focus that defined SEO don't apply to the emerging citation-based paradigm.
