## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. AI Expands Search Rather Than Replacing It

**INSIGHT:** "Most assume AI chatbots kill traditional search. AI is expansionary—it creates more queries. Google search traffic hasn't declined; instead, new question types (visual searches via Lens, complex multi-turn queries) generate growth opportunities alongside core search."

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Missing
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Vertigo

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 4
- Recognition: 2
- Implication: 5
- Specificity: 5

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Narrative / Cognitive

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Narrative Antimeme:** AI kills traditional search.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** New technology replaces old.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Prepare for decline.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Disruption narrative is strong
- Replacement is assumed
- Decline is expected
- The expansion is missed

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that AI kills search
- The belief that new replaces old
- The comfort of disruption framing

**Verification:**
> "AI is expansionary...there's actually just more and more questions being asked and curiosity that can be fulfilled now."

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Resource hard problems adequately** - Small teams take forever
2. **Copy formats that serve users** - Not inventing is obligation
3. **Measure emotional jobs** - Utility metrics miss value

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **AI expands search, not replaces it** - Creates more queries
2. **Don't stay lean too long** - Complex problems need resources
3. **Copying formats is product strategy** - Serve users, not originality

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Robby's antimemes share a structure: **product leadership at scale requires accepting counterintuitive truths about competition, resources, and measurement—AI expands search rather than replacing it because it creates new question types, don't stay lean too long on hard technical problems because undersized teams take forever, copying formats is product strategy because you're robbing users by not offering the best, and emotional jobs matter more than utility because metrics miss why people use products**. See expansion not disruption. Resource adequately. Copy what works. Measure emotional value. The pattern reveals that building Instagram and Google Search taught that product success comes from inversions that feel uncomfortable—where AI expands, lean hurts, copying serves, and emotions beat metrics.
