**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Organizational Antimeme:** Internal competition drives excellence.
- **System Antimeme:** Multiple bets increase odds.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Let teams compete.

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Prevent internal competition on acquisitions** - Cohesive vision required
2. **Watch for quiet team sabotage** - Resistance is organic and invisible
3. **Add async scaffolding to live products** - Pure sync isn't durable

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Internal competition kills acquisitions** - Cannibalization not excellence
2. **Success calcifies risk aversion** - Winners bet less, not more
3. **Live-only products aren't durable** - Need async scaffolding

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Kayvon's antimemes share a structure: **building products at Twitter and Periscope taught that organizational dynamics defeat product intuition—internal competition killed acquisitions rather than driving excellence, success at optimizing core calcified risk aversion rather than enabling bigger bets, teams sabotage quietly and organically without formal resistance, and live-only products aren't durable without asynchronous scaffolding**. Prevent internal competition. Watch for success calcification. Expect quiet sabotage. Add async to live. The pattern reveals that Twitter's product struggles came from organizational antimemes—where competition cannibalized, success prevented risk, resistance was invisible, and product purity was a trap.
