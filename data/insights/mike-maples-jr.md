## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Propose different futures not better execution** - Disorient incumbents
2. **Look for surprises not confirmation** - Discovery signal
3. **Pursue ambiguous opportunities** - Not big market problems

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Execution doesn't matter** - Different futures win
2. **80% of returns came from pivots** - Not initial ideas
3. **Don't solve big market problems** - Locks into incumbent categories

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Mike's antimemes share a structure: **venture success requires accepting that startup advice is inverted—execution doesn't matter because startups win by proposing different futures not executing better, 80% of biggest returns came from pivots not initial ideas, surprises are the discovery signal not hypothesis validation, and solving big market problems directly locks you into incumbent-defined categories**. Propose different futures. Expect pivots. Seek surprises. Pursue ambiguous. The pattern reveals that decades of early-stage investing taught that breakthrough companies come from inversions that feel wrong—where different beats better, pivots beat plans, surprises beat validation, and ambiguous beats clear.
