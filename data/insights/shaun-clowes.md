**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **System Antimeme:** AI disrupts incumbents.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** New technology creates new winners.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Build AI-first to compete.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- AI disruption narrative is dominant
- Startups want to believe in disruption

- The comfort of disruption narratives

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Don't assume AI disrupts your market** - Lock-in may protect incumbents
2. **Question revenue at validation moments** - Long-term obligations matter
3. **Expect worse distribution, not better** - AI increases noise

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **AI strengthens enterprise SaaS incumbents** - Not disruption
2. **Kill products when customers pay** - Validation as warning signal
3. **Scribble-shaped careers compound** - Non-specialization wins

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Shaun's antimemes share a structure: **enterprise product leadership requires accepting counterintuitive positions about AI, validation, and careers—AI strengthens incumbents rather than disrupting them, customer payment can signal unsustainable obligations rather than validation, scribble-shaped careers compound more than specialized ones, and distribution becomes harder not easier with AI tools**. Question disruption narratives. Kill validated products. Diversify your career. Expect worse distribution. The pattern reveals that the CPO of an enterprise company sees the opposite of startup conventional wisdom—where incumbents win, payments warn, breadth beats depth, and AI worsens rather than improves growth channels.
