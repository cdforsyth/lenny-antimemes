**Source:** Lenny's Podcast - Zoelle Egner Interview (Former Head of Growth at Airtable)
**Analyzed:** 2026-01-20

### Popular Takeaways (80-90% of listeners would mention)

1. **Growth requires strategy** - Plan your approach
2. **Product-led growth works** - Let product drive adoption
3. **Customer success matters** - Support users

6. **Champions drive adoption** - Find internal advocates

### Rare/Antimemetic Insights (1-5% would notice)

## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. Customer Success Should Precede Sales

**INSIGHT:** "Airtable hired customer success before establishing a sales team—utterly unconventional. Most assume you need sales first to generate revenue, but investing heavily in early customer success creates organic expansion and word-of-mouth that makes later sales effortless. Many people think Airtable was pure product led growth, and missed that huge customer success component."

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Missing
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Vertigo

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 5
- Recognition: 2
- Implication: 5
- Specificity: 5

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Organizational / Strategic

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Organizational Antimeme:** Sales comes first.
- **Strategic Antimeme:** Revenue before support.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Hire sales to grow.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Sales is prioritized
- Revenue is first
- Hiring sales is standard
- The CS-first path is missed

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that sales comes first
- The belief that revenue precedes support
- The comfort of standard hiring order

**Verification:**
> "Many people think Airtable was pure product led growth, and missed that huge customer success component."

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Hire customer success before sales** - Creates organic expansion
2. **Stop expecting PR to generate users** - Only helps hiring and outreach
3. **Target champions not buyers** - Non-decision-makers evangelize

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Customer success precedes sales** - CS-first creates effortless later sales
2. **PR doesn't generate users** - Only helps hiring credibility
3. **Champions matter more than buyers** - Overinvest in non-decision-makers

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Zoelle's antimemes share a structure: **growth requires accepting counterintuitive truths about hiring order, PR value, and sales targets—customer success should precede sales because early CS creates organic expansion that makes later sales effortless, PR and launches don't generate users because the value lies narrowly in hiring and cold outreach only, champions matter more than buyers because non-decision-makers who evangelize spread adoption internally, and repetition is leadership's core function because people are never listening as much as you think**. Hire CS first. Expect nothing from PR. Find champions. Repeat constantly. The pattern reveals that growing Airtable taught that growth comes from inversions that feel backward—where support precedes sales, PR is narrow, non-buyers matter, and repetition is essential.
