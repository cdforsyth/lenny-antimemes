## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Demonstrate investment before candor** - Care enables honesty
2. **Pair data with judgment** - Don't let research replace intuition
3. **Untether vision from technical constraints** - Not bound by today

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Genuine care over likability** - Different leadership goal
2. **Vision untethered from constraints** - Counter to realistic planning
3. **Data doesn't replace judgment** - Intuition alongside research

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Ebi's antimemes share a structure: **effective product leadership requires prioritizing what feels less professional—genuine care over likability despite care being harder to perform, vision untethered from technical constraints despite constraints seeming practical, judgment alongside data despite data seeming more rigorous, and demonstrated investment before candor despite feedback frameworks not mentioning prerequisites**. Care, don't seek likability. Vision beyond constraints. Trust judgment with data. Invest before candor. The pattern reveals that product leadership effectiveness comes from the less tangible, harder-to-measure elements—where the "soft" prerequisites of care and judgment enable the "hard" outputs of honest feedback and bold vision.
