## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Track D5/D7, not DAU** - Depth of usage over breadth
2. **Design for feelings explicitly** - Joy and speed as targets
3. **Vary leadership by project type** - No standardized hierarchy

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Feelings over metrics** - Different optimization target entirely
2. **Trust > transparency** - Transparency is tactic, trust is goal
3. **Browser as internet computer** - Not just a browser

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Josh's antimemes share a structure: **consumer product excellence requires optimizing for what metrics can't capture—feelings over graphs, usage depth over breadth, trust-building over transparency, and organizational flexibility over consistent hierarchy—because the brands people love (Nike, Apple, Disney) operate by different rules than the metrics-driven playbook teaches**. Design for joy. Measure depth. Build trust. Vary structure. The pattern reveals that competing with Chrome requires rejecting the metrics-first approach that dominates tech—where the unmeasurable qualities (how it feels, depth of commitment, earned trust) matter more than the measurable ones.
