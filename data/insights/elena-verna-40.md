**SCORES:**

- Recognition: 2

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Temporal / System

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Temporal Antimeme:** PMF is an achievement to maintain.
- **System Antimeme:** Product-market fit is a milestone.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Find PMF, then scale.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- PMF is taught as a destination
- "Find PMF" implies permanence
- The milestone framing is universal
- 3 months is exhausting to contemplate

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that PMF is achieved once
- The belief that scaling follows PMF
- The comfort of milestone-based planning

**Verification:**
> "Maintaining PMF now requires recapturing it every three months."

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Reframe free product as marketing cost** - Not margin erosion
2. **Hire for agency over experience** - Ex-founders, new grads
3. **Spend 95% on innovation** - Not optimization

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **PMF is a 3-month treadmill** - Not a destination
2. **Passionate problem-solvers over credentials** - Against hiring norms
3. **95% innovation vs. 5% optimization** - Inverted allocation

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Elena's Lovable antimemes share a structure: **AI-era growth requires abandoning the steady-state assumptions of traditional SaaS—product-market fit requires recapture every 3 months rather than being a milestone achieved, passionate problem-solvers matter more than credentials despite hiring norms, product distribution should be treated as marketing cost rather than margin erosion, and growth requires 95% innovation vs. 5% in previous roles**. Plan for perpetual PMF pursuit. Hire agency over experience. Give product away strategically. Innovate constantly. The pattern reveals that AI-era growth operates under fundamentally different physics—where the assumptions about permanence, credentials, monetization, and optimization that worked in traditional SaaS become actively harmful in a world where the underlying technology shifts quarterly.
