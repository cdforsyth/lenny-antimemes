## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. The Five-Second Moment Framework

**INSIGHT:** "Every compelling story pivots on a single moment of transformation. The actual story is just the flip itself—most speakers waste time on context."

**CLASSIFICATION:** Diagnostic

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Missing
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Recognition

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 5
- Recognition: 3
- Implication: 4
- Specificity: 5

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Implementation / Cognitive

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 8

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Set up the context thoroughly.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Stories need background.
- **System Antimeme:** More detail equals better story.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Context setup is taught
- Background seems necessary
- Detail seems valuable
- The flip is undervalued

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that context matters
- The belief that background helps
- The comfort of thorough setup

**Verification:**
> Every compelling story pivots on a single moment of transformation or realization. "Our minds are not designed to remember pie charts or facts...not attached to imagery." Most speakers waste time on context; the actual story is just the flip itself.

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Matthew's antimemes share a structure: **effective storytelling requires accepting that stories are everywhere and simpler than assumed—the five-second moment framework shows the actual story is just the flip, Homework for Life reveals 7.6 moments per day in ordinary life, nostalgia and adjacency trump direct messaging because theme beats content, and 98% of speaker anxiety happens before you begin**. Focus on the flip. Record daily moments. Use adjacent themes. Know anxiety is pre-talk. The pattern reveals that storytelling coaching taught that stories come from abundance and simplicity not scarcity and complexity—where less context beats more, ordinary beats dramatic, adjacent beats direct, and pre-talk beats performance.
