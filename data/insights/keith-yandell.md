- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Domain knowledge is required.

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Hire generalists for 10x outcomes** - Experts do business-as-usual
2. **Test with operations before product** - Streets before software
3. **Manufacture urgency continuously** - Compound small gains

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Crises increase employee satisfaction** - Singularity of focus
2. **Generalists outperform experts** - For 10x outcomes
3. **Trade dilution for trust** - Conservative projections

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Keith's antimemes share a structure: **building DoorDash required counterintuitive inversions of professional management wisdom—generalists outperform experts for 10x outcomes because fresh eyes reinvent, crises increase employee satisfaction because focus clarifies, founders manufacture urgency intentionally to compound small gains, and operations-first testing with promo codes beats building product integrations**. Hire generalists. Embrace crises. Move goalposts. Test manually. The pattern reveals that 7 years at DoorDash taught that high-growth execution inverts professional management—where inexperience beats expertise, crises beat stability, manufactured urgency beats realistic timelines, and manual testing beats product building.
