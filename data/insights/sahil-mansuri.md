## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. Deploy Top Salespeople as Customer Success Managers

**INSIGHT:** "Patch that leak really, really fast by protecting existing revenue while pursuing warm introductions from satisfied customers." The tactic: deploy top salespeople as customer success managers.

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Missing
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Vertigo

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 5
- Recognition: 2
- Implication: 5
- Specificity: 5

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** System / Organizational

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **System Antimeme:** Salespeople acquire, CS retains.
- **Organizational Antimeme:** Top performers should hunt.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Retention is a CS function.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Role definitions are fixed
- Top salespeople want to sell
- CS seems like demotion
- The revenue math isn't intuitive

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that sales and CS are separate
- The belief that top performers should hunt
- The comfort of fixed role definitions

**Verification:**
> "Deploying top salespeople as customer success managers."

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Put top salespeople on retention** - Patch the leak
2. **Use text for introductions** - Creates accountability
3. **Create custom research** - Not generic pitches

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Top salespeople as CS managers** - Role inversion
2. **"Doesn't feel salesy"** - The standard for good sales
3. **Text over email for intros** - Medium changes behavior

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Sahil's antimemes share a structure: **recession-era sales success requires inverting the standard playbook—deploying top salespeople as customer success managers to patch retention leaks contradicts role definitions, text introductions maintain accountability better than professional email, custom research reports beat scalable pitches despite not scaling, and effective sales doesn't feel salesy despite sales being a distinct profession**. Retain with sellers. Text the intro. Customize heavily. Stop being salesy. The pattern reveals that downturn sales effectiveness comes from the role inversions, medium changes, unscalable approaches, and identity shifts that contradict the efficient, scalable, professional sales playbook that works in growth periods.
