## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. Talent Density Is the Prerequisite, Not One of Many

**INSIGHT:** "We can't really have any of the other aspects of the culture...if you don't start with high talent density." Talent density isn't a cultural value alongside others - it's the foundation everything else depends on.

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Ignoring
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Vertigo

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 4
- Recognition: 2
- Implication: 5
- Specificity: 4

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Cognitive / System

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Culture has multiple equal pillars.
- **System Antimeme:** Different companies emphasize different values.
- **Implementation Antimeme:** Start with mission/values, then hire.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Companies list multiple equal values
- "Talent density" sounds elitist
- The dependency relationship isn't obvious
- Most cultures are built around mission, not talent

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that culture pillars are independent
- The belief that mission comes before talent
- The comfort of egalitarian cultural messaging

**Verification:**
> "We can't really have any of the other aspects of the culture...if you don't start with high talent density."

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Treat one-on-ones as sacred** - Genuine presence, not status updates
2. **Consider centralized specialist teams** - For objectivity, not just efficiency
3. **Frame freedom as talent requirement** - Not just employee benefit

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Talent density is prerequisite to all else** - Not one of many pillars
2. **Non-prescriptive approach requires exceptional people** - Freedom raises the bar
3. **Centralization enables objectivity** - Counter to embedded-team orthodoxy

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Elizabeth's antimemes share a structure: **Netflix's culture works because its distinctive practices are dependencies, not independent choices—talent density must come first before freedom and responsibility can work, centralized teams enable objectivity that embedded teams can't provide, non-prescriptive management requires rather than rewards exceptional people, and one-on-ones function as sacred time rather than process compliance**. Talent first. Centralize for independence. Freedom requires excellence. Protect the human moments. The pattern reveals that Netflix's culture isn't a menu of practices to adopt - it's an interlocking system where each element depends on the others, starting with the uncomfortable foundation of only hiring exceptional people.
