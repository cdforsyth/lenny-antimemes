## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. Growth Hacking Is Premature for Pre-Product Companies

**INSIGHT:** Sophisticated analytics, A/B testing, and growth hacking frameworks are premature for pre-product companies. These tactics suit later-stage startups with existing scale, not founders seeking their first customers. The growth toolbox assumes you have something that already works.

- Recognition vs. vertigo? Recognition

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Epistemic / Social

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 8

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Epistemic Antimeme:** Growth frameworks are taught as universal.
- **Social Antimeme:** Data-driven sounds professional and modern.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Measuring feels like progress.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Growth advice is widely published and shared
- Analytics dashboards feel productive
- "Data-driven" is a badge of sophistication
- The applicability conditions aren't discussed

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that growth frameworks apply to all stages
- The belief that measurement is always valuable
- The comfort of familiar tactics

**Verification:**
> "Sophisticated analytics, A/B testing, and growth hacking frameworks are premature for pre-product companies."

### 2. Investors Reject Because of Better Alternatives

**INSIGHT:** Investors reject opportunities not because of fatal flaws but because they possess better alternatives. Put yourself in investors' shoes - with limited capital and numerous options, they naturally pursue highest-confidence bets. This reframes rejection from "what's wrong with me" to "they have different options."

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Missing
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Recognition

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 4
- Recognition: 3

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Cognitive

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 8

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Rejection feels like evaluation of worth.
- **Social Antimeme:** Investors don't explain their full decision process.
- **Epistemic Antimeme:** "Improving the pitch" focuses on fixing flaws.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Rejection is emotionally processed as judgment
- Investors often give feedback that implies fixable problems
- The comparison set is invisible

**What's lost by accepting it:**

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Skip growth hacking pre-product** - Not applicable to early stage
2. **Reframe investor rejection** - Better alternatives, not fatal flaws
3. **Identify tarpit ideas** - Validation can be misleading

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Conviction comes from execution** - Not required before starting
2. **Honor in strategic retreat** - Nuanced perseverance advice
3. **Tarpit validation trap** - Positive feedback doesn't indicate viability

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Dalton's antimemes share a structure: **the signals that feel like guidance (investor rejection, user validation, early conviction) often mislead because they're interpreted through the wrong frame**. Rejection isn't about flaws; it's about alternatives. Validation isn't about viability; it's about immediate interest. Conviction isn't a starting condition; it's an output of execution. The pattern reveals that startup signals require translation - the obvious interpretation is usually wrong because the actual mechanism (investor choice set, structural market problems, conviction development) is invisible.
