## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Separate diagnosis from treatment** - Data ≠ solution
2. **Articulate success metrics precisely** - For humans and AI
3. **Build observability before instinct fails** - Growth eventually stops

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **PM role can be eliminated** - Skills distributed, not specialized
2. **Instinct-driven success has limits** - Observability becomes critical
3. **Diagnose with data, treat with design** - Distinct functions

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Julie's antimemes share a structure: **management in the AI era requires accepting limitations that fast growth obscures—diagnosing with data and treating with design are distinct functions despite data-driven framing, PM roles can be eliminated entirely by distributing skills rather than specializing, instinct-driven success has limits that appear when things stop growing, and vagueness undermines both humans and AI despite seeming empowering**. Separate diagnosis from treatment. Consider eliminating roles. Build observability. Be precise. The pattern reveals that design leadership at scale requires the precision, observability, and role flexibility that growth-mode intuition doesn't demand—where data and design separation, role elimination, measurement systems, and articulation precision matter more as companies mature and as AI becomes a teammate.
