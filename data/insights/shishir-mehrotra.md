- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Important questions should come first.
- **System Antimeme:** Eisenhower matrix is the default.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- Urgency/importance is the standard framework
- Leverage requires upstream analysis

- The comfort of familiar prioritization systems

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Prioritize by leverage (eigenquestions), not urgency** - Different prioritization system
2. **Invest more in reference checks** - Superior for P and S assessment
3. **Use neutral-court interview questions** - Test actual thinking

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **Eigenquestions over urgent/important** - Leverage as prioritization principle
2. **Rituals as products for employees** - Product mindset on culture
3. **References beat interviews for key dimensions** - Inversion of hiring wisdom

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Shishir's antimemes share a structure: **the standard approaches to decision-making, hiring, and culture (urgency prioritization, interview-centric assessment, culture as abstract values) miss the leverage points that actually determine outcomes**. Eigenquestions unlock cascading answers. References assess what interviews can't. Rituals are the actual cultural product. The pattern reveals that effectiveness comes from finding leverage—the single question, check, or ritual that unlocks disproportionate downstream value.
