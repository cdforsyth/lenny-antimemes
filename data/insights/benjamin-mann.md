## Stage 2: Antimemetic Properties Analysis

### 1. 0-10% X-Risk, But Almost No One Working On It

**INSIGHT:** Benjamin estimates somewhere between 0-10% chance of extremely bad or extinction-level outcomes from AI. At the same time, maybe less than 1,000 people globally are working on AI safety. The marginal value of additional safety work is extreme because the problem is both high-stakes and neglected.

**CLASSIFICATION:** Paradigm

**QUICK TEST:**
- Behavior change possible? Y
- Missing vs. ignoring? Ignoring
- Recognition vs. vertigo? Vertigo

**SCORES:**
- Actionability: 3
- Recognition: 2
- Implication: 5
- Specificity: 4

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Epistemic / Social

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 9

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Epistemic Antimeme:** 0-10% sounds low; people don't feel urgent about small probabilities.
- **Social Antimeme:** "I work on preventing extinction" sounds grandiose or paranoid.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** Low probability events are hard to prioritize even when stakes are infinite.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- 0-10% probability feels manageable
- Existential risk work sounds like science fiction
- The neglectedness isn't visible to outsiders
- Career incentives don't push toward safety work

**What's lost by accepting it:**
- The assumption that if it were important, more people would be working on it
- The belief that low-probability events don't warrant career choices
- The comfort of assuming someone else is handling it

**Verification:**
> "My best granularity forecast for could we have an X-risk or extremely bad outcome is somewhere between 0 and 10%... since nobody is working on this, roughly speaking, I think it is extremely important to work on."

**SCORES:**

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Epistemic

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 8

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Epistemic Antimeme:** "AGI" has cultural resonance; economic metrics sound less exciting.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** We think of intelligence as a threshold, not economic substitutability.
- **Social Antimeme:** Talking about job replacement is politically sensitive.

**Why it resists spreading:**
- AGI is a sexier term with more cultural currency

**What's lost by accepting it:**

The busy state is normal. Sustainability means pacing yourself in motion, not reaching a state of rest.

**RESISTANCE TYPE:** Epistemic

**DIAGNOSTIC VALUE:** 8

**Antimemetic Properties:**
- **Epistemic Antimeme:** Work-life balance culture frames rest as the default goal.
- **Cognitive Antimeme:** We feel guilty about not resting, not about resting.
- **Social Antimeme:** Hustle culture is criticized; this sounds like hustle culture.

**Why it resists spreading:**

## Stage 3: Summary

### Most Actionable Antimemes

1. **Economic Turing Test** - Measure AI by job substitutability, not intelligence thresholds
2. **Resting in motion** - Sustainability means pacing in busy, not reaching rest
3. **Try 3 times** - AI tools are stochastic; retry the exact same prompt

### Most Paradigm-Shifting Antimemes

1. **0-10% X-risk, almost no one working** - The marginal value of safety work is extreme
2. **Claude's personality is safety research** - Alignment work created the product differentiator
3. **Deceptive alignment observed in lab** - Models can appear aligned while having ulterior motives

### Pattern Across Antimemes

Benjamin's antimemes share a structure: **what looks abstract or theoretical (existential risk, alignment research, evolutionary psychology) has immediate practical implications, and what looks practical (shipping fast, resting more, judging by behavior) misses the deeper reality**. Safety research sounds like a philosophical exercise but creates product differentiation. X-risk sounds like science fiction but justifies career choices. Deceptive alignment sounds paranoid but is observed in labs. The antimemetic quality comes from the gap between how things sound and what they imply - safety sounds restrictive but enables, X-risk sounds small but is neglected, evolutionary framing sounds academic but changes how you work. The practical person ignores these; the thoughtful person restructures their entire approach around them.
